Atl Heavy Duty Towing provides professional light and medium duty towing services for passenger vehicles, SUVs, pickup trucks, cargo vans, and commercial vehicles up to 26,000 lbs. Whether you need a tow for your Honda Accord, your Ford F-150 work truck, or your Sprinter delivery van, our fleet of light and medium duty wreckers is equipped to handle the job safely and efficiently. We serve Atlanta, Sandy Springs, Marietta, Decatur & East Point, and all of Metro Atlanta with fast response times and experienced operators who treat every vehicle with care.Light duty towing covers most passenger vehicles—sedans, coupes, SUVs, crossovers, and small pickup trucks. For these vehicles, we typically deploy flatbed trucks because keeping all four wheels off the ground eliminates risk to the drivetrain, suspension, and tires. This is especially important in Atlanta, where potholes on older streets like Ponce de Leon Avenue or Roswell Road can cause a wheel-lifted vehicle to bottom out or scrape. We use soft straps to secure vehicles to the bed, and every flatbed is equipped with a hydraulic tilt mechanism that creates a gentle loading angle for low-clearance cars.Medium duty towing covers larger vehicles: box trucks, delivery vans, RVs, cargo vans, and commercial trucks with a gross vehicle weight rating up to 26,000 lbs. These vehicles require more powerful wreckers with higher-capacity booms, winches, and stabilizing outriggers. Our medium duty wrecker fleet is maintained in-house at our Atlanta facility, with regular hydraulic system checks, cable inspections, and brake testing to ensure safe operation under load. Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What vehicles qualify for light duty towing?

Light duty towing covers most passenger vehicles including cars, SUVs, crossovers, and small pickup trucks. Medium duty covers larger trucks, cargo vans, and vehicles up to 26,000 lbs.

Q: Do you use flatbed tow trucks?

Yes, we primarily use flatbed tow trucks which are the safest method for modern vehicles. Flatbed towing eliminates the risk of damage to the vehicle's drivetrain that can occur with traditional tow dollies.

Q: Can you tow my car from a parking garage or tight space?

Our fleet includes compact tow trucks specifically designed for tight spaces, parking garages, and areas with limited access. Describe your situation when you call and we'll send appropriate equipment.

Q: Do you offer long-distance towing services?

Yes, we provide long-distance towing throughout Georgia and can arrange transport to surrounding states. Our fleet and partnerships allow us to handle moves of any distance efficiently.

Q: What areas do you serve for light duty towing?

We serve Atlanta, Sandy Springs, Roswell, Sandy Springs, Lithonia, Decatur, Smyrna, Tucker, and all surrounding Metro Atlanta and South Atlanta communities.
